Every student has something to teach.

SkillSwap is a student-centred platform for peer-to-peer learning on campus. Students teach the skills they have, learn the ones they don't, and grow together through a supportive community.

Our vision

A campus where students exchange knowledge, improve how they communicate, and build practical skills beyond the classroom.

What we offer

Ten skills to begin with, including coding, photography, public speaking, design and music, with mentors who have done it themselves.

Why SkillSwap

It builds creativity, teamwork, confidence and leadership through real conversations between students, not lectures.

Our mission

To make learning collaborative, accessible and enjoyable. We believe every student has a skill worth sharing and something new to learn.
