How a swap happens

1

Pick your side

Sign up as a learner or a mentor. Learners choose what they want to learn, and mentors choose what they teach.

2

Send or accept a request

Learners ask a mentor for a session. When the mentor accepts, both sides get a meeting link.

3

Meet, then stay in touch

Connect with the people you liked and message them. Mentors collect points and badges for every session.

Your first swap is one sign-up away.
